Memorial slideshow service

A respectful photo tribute for a funeral, memorial, or celebration of life.

Dan can scan printed photos, combine them with digital pictures, add music, and create a ready-to-play slideshow for a service, gathering, or family keepsake. Memorial projects are quoted individually because timing, photo count, and editing needs vary.

What to send Dan

  • The service or event date.
  • A rough number of printed and digital photos.
  • Any preferred songs, tone, or family requests.
  • Whether the slideshow will play on a TV, projector, laptop, or funeral home system.

Planning guidance

Most tribute slideshows work best with 30-120 photos. If the deadline is close, Dan can help prioritize the images that matter most and keep the project realistic.

Printed photos can be scanned first, then blended with phone pictures, emailed photos, or images family members share digitally.
