How to prepare a shoebox project

  • Keep photos in the order you want scanned.
  • Use envelopes, rubber bands, or sticky notes to separate events or decades.
  • Remove tape, paper clips, staples, and sticky residue when safe.
  • Do not worry about perfect sorting. A rough grouping is usually enough.
