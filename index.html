Top Questions

Tap any question for the quick answer.

How long does scanning take?
Usually 5–10 business days depending on volume, plus shipping time if mailed.
Do you scan 35mm slides and negatives?
Yes! We scan 35mm slides (cardboard or plastic mounted) and 35mm negatives (film strips) at $0.35 each, with our $50 minimum on any combined order. We don’t scan 120, 110, or sheet film — for those formats, see our DIY Tools section for a recommended home scanner.
Can I drop off my photos?
Yes — just schedule with Dan first so you don’t arrive to a locked door (he works from a home studio). Pickup and drop-off are free in Hamburg, NY. For addresses within about a 40-mile radius, pickup is typically $10 and optional drop-off after scanning is another $10. Text Dan to set a time that works for you.
Do you return the original photos?
Absolutely. Your originals are carefully returned after digitizing — by pickup/drop-off locally, or by return shipping with tracking for mail-in orders.
Do you create photo slideshows?
Yes! We create custom photo slideshows with music for birthdays, memorials, anniversaries, military tributes, and more. We combine your old printed photos with digital images into a beautiful presentation. Learn more or contact us for a quote.
