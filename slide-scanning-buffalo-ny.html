What Dan should know before quoting

A rough slide count is enough to start. If you have carousels, boxes, or mixed slides and prints, text a quick picture. Dan can help decide whether to preserve the current order, group by tray, or combine slides with printed photos in one project.

Do you scan other film sizes?

Slide scanning is for 35mm slides in cardboard or plastic mounts. 120, 110, and sheet film aren't scanned. For 35mm film strips, see 35mm negative scanning. Text Dan if you're not sure what you have.

Can I drop slides off near Buffalo?

Yes, local drop-off and pickup are available by appointment in the Hamburg/Buffalo area. Customers farther away can use mail-in scanning. See also photo scanning in Buffalo.
