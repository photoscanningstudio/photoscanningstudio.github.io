Pickup and drop-off are free in the Hamburg and South Buffalo area, and $10 each way elsewhere in Western New York. The $50 minimum applies to your combined scanning total. Polaroids and larger prints are priced like regular photo prints. Most projects take 5–10 days, about a week on average.

Plan a convenient appointment

Start with a rough count or a photo of your collection. Dan will confirm a time to meet at the Hamburg home studio, explain the work, and answer your questions before you leave your originals.

No need to remove stuck or fragile photos from albums first. Album removal, hand-scanning, and extra-large albums are assessed separately so you know the cost before work begins.

Simple local process

  • Text or call first so Dan can confirm timing and project details.
  • Bring loose photos, albums, slides, negatives, or a whole family photo box.
  • Dan scans photo prints at 600 DPI, includes an enhanced copy, and returns your originals.
  • Choose free cloud delivery, a $20 USB, or bring your own USB to load.

Why appointment-based matters

This is a home studio, not a walk-in retail counter. Scheduling first means Dan is ready for you, can look over the project carefully, and can answer questions about albums, fragile prints, order, turnaround, and delivery.

Hamburg pickup and drop-off are available by appointment, with mail-in service available for family members outside Western New York.
