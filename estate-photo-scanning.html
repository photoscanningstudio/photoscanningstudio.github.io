Estate photo scanning

Preserve inherited photos before the boxes get split up, stored, or forgotten.

When a parent passes, a home is sold, or a family member downsizes, photos can become one of the hardest things to handle. Dan helps families scan inherited prints, albums, slides, and negatives so everyone can keep a digital copy.

Ask About an Estate ProjectText Dan

Common estate projects

  • Boxes of loose photos found in a basement, attic, closet, or storage unit.
  • Albums that need to be preserved before they are divided among siblings.
  • Slides and negatives from vacations, holidays, weddings, and family milestones.
  • Memorial slideshow projects for services or celebration-of-life events.

A practical way to start

You do not have to solve the whole archive first. Text Dan a few photos of the boxes, albums, or shelves. He can suggest a first pass, such as scanning the best loose photos first, preserving album order, or separating a memorial slideshow from a larger archive.
