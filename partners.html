How it works

1

You get a card with your name on it

I print a small stack of cards that say "Compliments of [your business]" and drop them off. They sit by your register, in the folder you hand clients, or in the truck.

2

Your customer gets 15% off

Anyone who shows me one of your cards gets 15% off whatever I quote them, on top of any special I'm running. The discount reads as coming from you, because it does.

3

I send work back to you

You get listed on this page with a link to your site, and when a customer of mine needs framing, a cleanout, an estate sale, or a photographer, I point them at the people on this list first.

There is no contract, no fee either direction, no minimum, and nothing to track. If it stops being useful, tell me and I'll take you off the list the same day.

Common questions

Does it cost anything? No. Not to join, not per referral, not ever. I don't pay referral fees either, in either direction. The whole thing is the 15% going to your customer instead.

Do I have to send you anything? No. Most partners never send a single person, and that is genuinely fine. The cards sit there until somebody needs one.

What do you actually do? Photo prints, 35mm slides and negatives, Polaroids, restoration of faded and damaged prints, and slideshows set to music for birthdays, anniversaries, and memorial services. Originals always come back.

What if my customer's photos are ruined? Water-damaged prints are often more recoverable than they look. I will tell someone honestly when they aren't, rather than take the job.

Are you going to email me constantly? No. If you tell me it isn't your thing, I won't write again.
