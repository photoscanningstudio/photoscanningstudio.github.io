Negatives are priced per frame, so 143 or more frames reach the $50 minimum on their own. You can also combine negatives with prints and slides in one order. Most projects take 5–10 days, about a week on average.

Not sure what type of film you found?

Leave film in its sleeves and send Dan a clear picture. The service covers 35mm film-strip negatives; 120, 110, and sheet film are not accepted. Do not cut strips or try to remove damage before asking.

Your original film is returned. Free private cloud delivery and optional $20 USB delivery make the images easier to share. Tell Dan about any specific resolution or printing needs before booking.

What negative formats are accepted?

  • 35mm film strip negatives are $0.35 each.
  • The $50 minimum applies to any combination of photos, slides, and negatives.
  • 120, 110, and sheet film are not currently scanned in-house.
  • Keep negatives in sleeves if you have them; do not cut or clean them aggressively.

Best next step

If you are not sure what type of film you have, text Dan a close photo of the strips next to a quarter or ruler. He can tell you whether it looks like 35mm and whether it belongs in the project.

Questions about negative scanning

Can I drop negatives off near Buffalo?

Yes, by appointment. Pickup and drop-off are free in the Hamburg and South Buffalo area, and $10 each way elsewhere in Western New York. Customers farther away can use mail-in scanning.

Can I mix negatives with prints and slides?

Yes. Everything goes in one order and counts toward the same $50 minimum. See photo scanning in Buffalo and slide scanning.
