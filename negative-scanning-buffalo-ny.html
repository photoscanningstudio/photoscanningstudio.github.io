What negative formats are accepted?

  • 35mm film strip negatives are $0.35 each.
  • The $50 minimum applies to any combination of photos, slides, and negatives.
  • 120, 110, and sheet film are not currently scanned in-house.
  • Keep negatives in sleeves if you have them; do not cut or clean them aggressively.

Best next step

If you are not sure what type of film you have, text Dan a close photo of the strips next to a quarter or ruler. He can tell you whether it looks like 35mm and whether it belongs in the project.
