How Buffalo customers usually start

Text Dan a quick picture of your boxes, albums, slides, or envelopes. You don't need an exact count. He can usually give a practical next step: whether to leave the photos in order, whether albums need extra handling, and whether local pickup or drop-off makes the most sense.

Do you come to Buffalo?

Yes, local pickup and drop-off are available by appointment around Buffalo and Erie County. Dan confirms availability and any pickup cost before booking. Customers farther away can use mail-in photo scanning.

Can I mix photos, slides, and negatives?

Yes. They go in one order and count toward the same $50 minimum. See slide scanning and negative scanning for details.

Will my photos stay in order?

Tell Dan how your photos are grouped and whether the order matters, and he'll talk through the best way to handle it before scanning.
